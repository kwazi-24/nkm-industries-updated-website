Privacy Notice

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This notice explains how NKM INDUSTRIES Holding Company (Pty) Ltd (“NKM”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information that you give us through this website, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

If you use the enquiry form, we ask for your name, email address, an optional phone number, the service you are interested in, and your message. We only collect what you choose to type in.

How the enquiry form works

This website does not store form submissions on a server. When you press “Send via WhatsApp”, the details you entered are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device, and you decide whether to send it. If you choose the email option instead, the details are placed in an email on your own device. Once a message reaches us through WhatsApp or email, it is handled under those services’ own privacy terms as well as this notice.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote.
  • To deliver the service you ask for, if you become a client.

We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers we need to deliver your request (for example, payment or hosting providers once you become a client), and where the law requires it.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not set tracking or advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google’s servers when you visit. Links to WhatsApp open a service run by WhatsApp.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages for as long as needed to deal with your request and our business records, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to us using it. Email info@nkmindustries.co.za and we will respond. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.
